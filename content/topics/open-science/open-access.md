## What open access means

Open access means that research results can be read by anyone, free of charge and without technical barriers. Readers do not need a subscription, and authors usually keep the right to share and reuse their own work.

## Ways to publish open access

- **Gold:** the article is free to read in the journal itself, often paid for by a publication fee.
- **Green:** the authors place a version of the article in a repository, sometimes after an embargo.
- **Diamond:** the journal is free for readers and for authors.
