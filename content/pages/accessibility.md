The University of Lübeck strives to make this website of the Open Science Initiative Lübeck accessible, in accordance with the Schleswig-Holstein Act on Equal Opportunities for Persons with Disabilities (Landesbehindertengleichstellungsgesetz, LBGG) and the Barrier-Free Information Technology Ordinance (BITV 2.0), which implement Directive (EU) 2016/2102.

This statement applies to this website.

## How accessible this website is

This website is **partially compliant** with the requirements of BITV 2.0 and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

The website was built with accessibility in mind: it can be used with the keyboard, works at every screen size and when zoomed in, uses sufficient colour contrast and a clear structure of headings, and gives text descriptions for pictures where they carry information.

## What is not yet accessible

- **Sign language and Easy Language:** explanations of the website in German Sign Language and in Easy Language (Leichte Sprache) are not yet available.
- **Pictures and documents added later:** pictures, documents and texts are added by different people over time. Some of them may lack a text description or may not be fully accessible.
- **Language:** the website is in English. Some names, titles and quotations are in German.
- **Other websites and documents** that this website links to are not covered by this statement.

We are working to remove these barriers step by step.

## How this statement was prepared

This statement was prepared on 17 November 2026. It is based on a self-assessment: all pages were checked with an automated testing tool (axe, against WCAG 2.1 level AA), and the layout was checked at different screen widths. The statement will be reviewed once a year and when the website changes substantially.

## Feedback and contact

If you notice barriers on this website, or need information from it in another form, please write to us: [open.science@uni-luebeck.de](mailto:open.science@uni-luebeck.de). We will reply as soon as we can.

## Complaints procedure

If you do not receive a satisfactory answer from us, you can contact the complaints office under the LBGG at the Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities of Schleswig-Holstein. It helps to resolve conflicts about accessibility between people with disabilities and public bodies in Schleswig-Holstein, free of charge.
