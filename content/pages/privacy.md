## In short

- This website sets **no cookies** and uses **no analytics or tracking**.
- Everything on it, including fonts, scripts and pictures, comes from this website itself. No content from other providers is loaded.
- A small script in your browser opens the menu on small screens and hides dates that have passed. It sends nothing anywhere.

## Contacting us by e-mail

E-mail links on this website open your own e-mail program. If you write to us, we process your e-mail address, your message and any other details you give, only to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with the data protection law of Schleswig-Holstein, as part of the university's public tasks. We delete your message when it is no longer needed, unless rules on keeping records require otherwise.

## The editing area

The area at `/admin/` is used only by the people who edit this website. Logging in takes place with their account at the code hosting service where the website's files are kept (GitHub or GitLab). After logging in, an access key is kept in the editor's browser until they log out. Each change is recorded in the website's version history with the editor's account name and the time of the change.

## Links to other websites

This website links to other websites. When you follow such a link, the other provider is responsible for the processing of your data there.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to

- access to the data stored about you (Art. 15),
- correction of incorrect data (Art. 16),
- erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18),
- object to processing (Art. 21),
- receive your data in a common format, where this applies (Art. 20).

To exercise these rights, please contact the data protection officer named above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For the University of Lübeck this is the Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein (ULD), [www.datenschutzzentrum.de](https://www.datenschutzzentrum.de).
