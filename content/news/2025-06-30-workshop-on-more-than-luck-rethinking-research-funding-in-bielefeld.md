### Workshop on “More than Luck: Rethinking Research Funding” in Bielefeld

From the 30th of June to the 3rd of July the OSI of the University of Luebeck will participate at the Workshop “More than Luck: Rethinking Research Funding”. In the next three days, in a series of workshops, talks and discussions the interdisciplinary group of selected researchers and funding organisations discuss urgent issues to improve research funding at the renowned ZiF at the University of Bielefeld [Wie kann Forschungsförderung besser werden? – Aktuell Uni Bielefeld](https://aktuell.uni-bielefeld.de/2025/06/23/wie-kann-forschungsfoerderung-besser-werden/).
