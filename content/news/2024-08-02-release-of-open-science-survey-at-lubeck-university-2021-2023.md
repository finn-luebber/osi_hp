#### Open Science Survey at Lübeck University

The Open Science Initiative conducted two surveys at the university in 2021 and 2023 to get an impression of how well university staff and students know various OS practices, what their attitudes towards the practices are, how often they use them, and what needs there are for more frequent use. If you are interested in the findings, please check out our [Materials](https://osi-luebeck.de/resources/materials/) page (Lübeck specific information).
