### Keynote at the 4th Helmholtz Reproducibility Workshop in Berlin

Prof. Frieder Paulus will represent the OSI of the University of Luebeck and provide a keynote on “Contextualization of reproducibility in the organization of academic work” at the [4\. Helmholtz Reproducibility Workshop](https://events.hifis.net/event/1750/) in Berlin. This year’s meeting, organized by the Helmholtz Open Science Office, provides a platform to discuss and work on issues of scientific integrity to shape reproducible, robust, and transparent research within the Helmholtz community and beyond.
