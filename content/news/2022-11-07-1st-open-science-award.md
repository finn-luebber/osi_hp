We are proud to have awarded the Lübeck Open Science Award for the first time! The first prize winners are [Lilian Kojan](https://www.imis.uni-luebeck.de/de/institut/team/lilian-kojan) and [André Calero Valdez](https://www.imis.uni-luebeck.de/de/institut/team/andre-calero-valdez) from the Institute for Multimedia and Interactive Systems. The awarded project is a published paper named “[Perceptions of behavior efficacy, not perceptions of threat, are drivers of COVID-19 protective behavior in Germany](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-022-01098-4)“, an investigation of the socio-psychological predictors of corona protective behavior. In addition to the publication as Open Access under cc-by 4.0 license, preregistration, Open Data and Open Code, the jury particularly highlighted the [website](https://digitalemuendigkeit.github.io/covid-19-behavior-sem/) created especially for the project containing alternative and further analyses, which is not yet standard regarding Open Science practices. [Here](https://www.uni-luebeck.de/aktuelles/die-preise-der-universitaet/open-science-preis.html) you can find the news on the website of the University of Lübeck.

We look forward to the second round of the award in 2023!
