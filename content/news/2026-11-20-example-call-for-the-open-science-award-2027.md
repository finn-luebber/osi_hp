With the Open Science Award of the University of Lübeck, we honour a project from research or teaching that makes science more open, transparent or reproducible.

## Who can apply

Individuals and groups at the University of Lübeck and the UKSH, Campus Lübeck. Nominations by others are welcome too.

## What to send

- a link to the project
- a short summary (200–250 words)
- a short explanation of why the project deserves the award

Please send your submission to [open.science@uni-luebeck.de](mailto:open.science@uni-luebeck.de) by **31 March 2027**.
