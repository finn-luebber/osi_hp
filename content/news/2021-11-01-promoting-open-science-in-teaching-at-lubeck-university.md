During the last two weeks we presented the background and perspective of the OSI Lübeck at various meetings of lecturers at the study programs of the [UzL](http://www.uni-luebeck.de/). The UzL offers many study programs from undergraduate and graduate studies that relate to Open Science practices. These include medicine, psychology, molecular life sciences and other interdisciplinary programs of the biomedical sciences. In some curricula open science issues have already been brought forward. We have been met with open ears and great interests in many disciplines. We are very much looking forward to the upcoming work on these issues and whether and how the very diverse curricula at the UzL programs allow to broaden the perspective of graduate and undergraduate students to these issues.
