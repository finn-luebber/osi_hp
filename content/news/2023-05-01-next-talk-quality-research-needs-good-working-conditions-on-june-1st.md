Dear Colleagues and Students,

Our next guest is Dr Rima-Maria Rahal, postdoc at the Max Planck Institute for Research on Collective Goods (Cologne). Rima-Maria Rahal will speak about her latest paper on why “[Quality research needs good working conditions](https://scholar.google.de/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&user=QaLBRJgAAAAJ&sortby=pubdate&citation_for_view=QaLBRJgAAAAJ:_kc_bZDykSQC)“, published in Nature Human Behavior. We are excited to discuss this important and timely topic on June 1st at 3PM.

The talk will be held in English and will take place in WebEx. Just email us if you are interested!
