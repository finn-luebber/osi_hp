### New series of Manuscripts of the primary outcome of SCORE program with participation from OSI of the University of Luebeck

We are happy to announce the primary outcomes of the SCORE program ([SCORE | Center for Open Science](https://www.cos.io/score)) to be finally released and published. In a series of manuscripts and preprints, this large-scale multi-method research initiative aimed at providing answers on the trustworthiness of research. More than 850 collaborators joined this effort evaluated 3,900 scientific claims. The collection is available in Nature and we are very excited to have contributed to this projects, together with so many others.

You can read more on this project here on the series in Nature [https://www.nature.com/collections/idajfifcfg](https://www.nature.com/collections/idajfifcfg) or on the website of the SCORE program [SCORE | Center for Open Science](https://www.cos.io/score).
