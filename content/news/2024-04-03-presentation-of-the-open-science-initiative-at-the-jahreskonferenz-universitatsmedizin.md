[Dr. Lena Rademacher](https://social-neuroscience-lab.com/dr-lena-rademacher/) presented the Open Science Initiative at the [Jahreskonferenz Universitätsmedizin](https://www.uksh.de/jahreskonferenz2024.html) in Neumünster. In a panel discussion, together with Professor Ulrich Dirnagl, Professor Thomas Münte, Professor Christian Herzog, Professor Ilka Parchmann and Professor Claudia Bozzaro, Lena outlined the Open Science perspective on “responsible research”.
