Dear colleagues, dear students,   
we would like to invite you to a talk on **Mai 19 at 3 pm** by [Clara Weber](https://social-neuroscience-lab.com/clara-weber/) (Lübeck University/McGill University) on “Berlin Exchange Medicine – A contribution of student journals to Open Science”

Clara Weber is an Open Science Officer and Editor in the Data and Statistics Section at BEM. She is studying medicine and medical informatics in Lübeck, Germany, is working on her PhD in the Social Neuroscience Lab, and spent the last year at Yale and McGill University.

The talk will be held in English and will take place in WebEx. Just email us if you are interested!  

We look forward to seeing you!  
Open Science Initiative Lübeck
