Today we had a very interesting meeting with the Responsible Metrics team of the BIH QUEST Center for Responsible Research. The meeting was organized by the German Reproducibility Network (GRN) to increase communication among initiatives that are part of the network and facilitate collaborations. The exchange with Responsible Metrics was very inspiring. Specifically their training of personnel involved in RCTs could be relevant for the many clinical research activities at the University of Lübeck and we will be happy to keep in touch.
