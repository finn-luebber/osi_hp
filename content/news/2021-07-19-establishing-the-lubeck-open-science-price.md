We are very happy to continue our work on the Open Science Price of the University of Lübeck. The board has agreed to support the establishment of the Open Science Price in the upcoming years, which has been made possible due to an anonymous donor. We are currently working on the formal regulations for the call and formation of the interdisciplinary panel.
