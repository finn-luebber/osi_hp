Dear colleagues, dear students,   
we would like to invite you to a talk on **January 27 at 3 pm** by Prof. [Sander van der Laan](https://www.umcutrecht.nl/en/research/researchers/van-der-laan-sander-sw) (Utrecht University) on ” Open Science and science in transition in Utrecht”

  
Sander van der Laan is a (medical) biologist and assistant professor at the University Medical Center Utrecht. His research interest is focused on the genetic causes of atherosclerosis underlying cardiovascular disease. In addition, he has a passion for Open Science and has become the first Chair to the Open Science Community Utrecht (for more information see [https://openscience-utrecht.com/a-new-year-a-new-chair/](https://openscience-utrecht.com/a-new-year-a-new-chair/)). 

The talk will be held in English and will take place in WebEx. Just email us if you are interested!  

We look forward to seeing you!  
Open Science Initiative Lübeck
