Dear Colleagues and Students,

Our next guest, this Wednesday, 12.07. at 12h, is **Dr Helena Hartmann**, postdoc at the Uniklinik Essen. Helena Hartmann will give a lecture with the title “**Open Science and feminist psychology – a dream team?**“. In it, she aims to discuss how feminist researchers navigate the world of (open) science, guided by the following questions: How can the goals of open science and feminist psychology influence each other? Why should we and how can we incorporate a feminist perspective into the way we do science? And what challenges and obstacles do feminist early career scientists need to be aware of?

The talk will be held in English and will take place in WebEx. Just email us if you are interested!
